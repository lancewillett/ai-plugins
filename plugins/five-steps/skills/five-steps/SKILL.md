# Five steps

Walk the target through all five steps, then edit it.

## Why this matters

The natural instinct is to automate first. The framework forces the harder questions earlier: do we even need this requirement? Can we delete this step entirely? Only after pruning and simplifying does automation make sense.

Scope: processes, tools, products, and systems you own.

## The framework

Adapted from Elon Musk's five-step "algorithm" for improving a process.

1. **Question every requirement**—Is this necessary? Who asked for it? Would we add it if starting fresh?
2. **Delete parts or processes**—Remove the unnecessary. Add things back 10% of the time, or you're not deleting enough.
3. **Simplify or optimize**—Make what remains easier to work with.
4. **Accelerate cycle time**—Speed up without sacrificing quality.
5. **Automate**—Last, not first.

## Application areas

### Shaping new plans

Use the 5 steps as a checklist when co-creating plans with AI or with the team. Before any plan gets a green light, walk it through all 5 steps.

### Reviewing existing systems

Periodically audit your systems against the framework:

- CI/CD pipelines—what steps can be deleted or simplified?
- Triage workflows—are we questioning requirements or just adding process?
- Testing infrastructure—what's over-engineered?
- Reporting and metrics—do we need all these dashboards?

## Make the changes

Edit the target in place: cut what step 2 deleted, rewrite what survived.
